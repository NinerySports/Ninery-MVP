# ADR: Real-World Equipment Behavioral Evaluation v1.0

## Status

Accepted for internal development workflow.

## Context

Ticket #045 onboarded the 2023 DeMarini The Goods (-10) USA into the catalog with objective product and variant specifications. It intentionally left required behavioral Equipment DNA unresolved: `swing_effort`, `forgiveness`, `sweet_spot_support`, and `bat_control_support`.

## Decision

Ninery will evaluate real-world behavioral Equipment DNA with an evidence-governed, ordinal-first workflow. The workflow may produce ordinal evaluations, numeric references, evidence gaps, conflicts, and readiness status, but it does not alter live recommendation scoring or Transition Compatibility.

## Intrinsic vs Relational Boundary

Equipment DNA describes characteristics of the equipment itself. Jackson-specific performance, acclimation, transition score, or Pilot Study #1 observations are Compatibility Intelligence or validation evidence. They must not define prospective intrinsic Equipment DNA for the bat being tested.

## Evidence Hierarchy

Evidence categories are:

- objective measured evidence
- manufacturer technical evidence
- independent expert evidence
- structured internal equipment evaluation
- anecdotal player-specific evidence
- marketing claim
- unknown

Objective measured and structured internal evidence may support numeric references when methodology is known. Independent evidence may support ordinal evaluation. Marketing and manufacturer claims are retained as evidence but do not create false precision.

## Evidence Independence

Correlated duplicate claims do not increase confidence. Independent source groups are required before confidence can rise meaningfully from corroboration.

## Conflict Handling

Conflicting ordinal evidence is preserved and blocks readiness until reviewed. The evaluator does not average qualitative claims or choose a favorable value.

## Ordinal-First Policy

The evaluator determines canonical ordinal meaning first using the Equipment DNA registry vocabulary. Numeric references require additional measured or structured evidence.

## Contamination Prevention

The workflow rejects retrospective validation evidence, player-specific observations, Pilot Study #1 outcome evidence, transition compatibility results, and post-prediction observations as prospective Equipment DNA inputs.

## Readiness Boundary

Catalog readiness, canonical Equipment DNA readiness, genuine-study readiness, and live recommendation activation remain separate. Ticket #046 does not activate DeMarini for live recommendations.

## DeMarini Outcome

With only Ticket #045 objective specification evidence, the DeMarini behavioral profile remains unresolved. The correct next step is structured physical evaluation or stronger independent behavioral evidence, not fabricated values.
