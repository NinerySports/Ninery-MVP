# External Expert Supporting-Evidence Role v1.0

Status: `PROVISIONAL_CONSTRUCT_SPECIFIC_POLICY`  
Version: `external_expert_supporting_role/1.0-provisional`

## Decision

External expert observations remain source-provenance records, not a seventh evidence class. A separate `supporting_context` role may be granted only to genuine, identity-safe external editorial observations mapped with high confidence to `startup_demand` or `rotational_demand`, from a known-independent lineage, after explicit human approval.

The policy was earned by Tickets #072 and #073. It is provisional, reversible, and construct-specific. All other Protocol v1.1 constructs remain supporting-ineligible.

## Evaluation order

1. Reject superseded or conflicting claims.
2. Reject marketing and non-behavioral claims.
3. Require `independent_expert_review` provenance.
4. Require `independent_observation` lineage.
5. Require resolved, applicable identity without promoting its scope.
6. Require an explicitly eligible construct and high-confidence mapping.
7. Accept the unchanged #070 `context_only` baseline or an already-qualified claim.
8. Require an explicit human `reviewed_accepted` or `reviewed_with_limitations` decision.
9. Assign `supporting_context` with zero direct, canonical, numeric, synthesis, or recommendation contribution.

Comparative observations retain their comparison target. Direction remains source-level interpretation; it never becomes a canonical ordinal or number. Multiple observations in one independence group count as one independent supporting source. Conflicts remain visible and are never resolved by majority vote. Superseded claims remain historical but inactive.

## Read models and sufficiency

`summarizeExternalExpertSupportingCoverage` is an additive companion to the #066 read model. It reports supporting records, lineage-aware source counts, and construct coverage without changing the six evidence-class buckets. The output always contributes zero direct evidence and grants no synthesis eligibility. Therefore supporting-only evidence cannot weaken #067 readiness.

Future trust language may say, "Independent reviewers describe this bat as easier to get moving." It must not say Ninery measured, proved, or validated that behavior.

## Automation boundaries

- `READY_FOR_AUTOMATION`: deterministic role assignment after reviewed inputs; duplicate grouping by established lineage.
- `AI_ASSISTED_REVIEW_REQUIRED`: source classification, dependency research, identity candidates, construct mapping, mapping-confidence suggestions, marketing detection.
- `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`: identity acceptance, independence acceptance, construct mapping acceptance, supporting-role approval.
- `NOT_READY`: canonical synthesis, numeric conversion, causal claims, majority-vote truth resolution.

## Persistence boundary

Ticket #074 is domain-only. The current research fixtures are simulated in memory and are not persisted or retroactively approved. Durable production storage should later preserve source, document, author, claim, identity scope, dependency group, construct relationship, mapping confidence, human approval, evidence role, policy version, conflict, and supersession semantics. No Prisma change is made here.

## Expansion and revocation

Expanding beyond the two eligible constructs requires additional cross-product calibration, response-specific evidence, and where appropriate controlled mechanical validation or a better vibration ontology. A later policy version may add or remove constructs or tighten identity, independence, and review gates without rewriting historical provenance.
